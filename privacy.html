Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how SiteSnag handles information. It applies to the SiteSnag iPhone and iPad app published by Limitless Technology Solutions Limited, trading as Accelerate IT, in the United Kingdom.

We do not collect personal data from the app

SiteSnag collects no personal data. There are no accounts, and the app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.

All projects, photos, voice notes, room scans and signatures are stored only on your device. We do not operate a SiteSnag server that receives this content, and we cannot see your inspections.

Permissions you can choose to use

Camera, photo library, microphone and speech recognition are used only when you choose to capture or dictate something, for example to photograph a defect, attach an existing photo, or log a snag by voice. You can refuse a permission in iOS settings; the rest of the app continues to work without that feature.

Speech recognition

Voice snags and dictated notes use Apple’s Speech framework. Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed on the device or sent to Apple’s servers to be converted into text. That processing is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy, not to any service run by us. We do not receive that audio.

When information is shared

SiteSnag shares data only when you choose to export or share a PDF report yourself, for example by email or through the iOS share sheet. What happens to a file after you share it is under your control, and is subject to the service or person you send it to.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the SiteSnag app deletes your SiteSnag data from that device. Copies you have already exported, shared or included in a device backup are not removed by deleting the app.

Children

SiteSnag is a business inspection app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

UK GDPR and how to contact us

Limitless Technology Solutions Limited, trading as Accelerate IT, is the organisation responsible for this policy and is the data controller for any personal data you send us directly, for example if you email or telephone support. We use those contact details only to respond to your request.

Because the app itself does not collect personal data, we do not ordinarily hold personal data from your use of SiteSnag. If you have a question about this policy, or wish to exercise a right under UK GDPR in relation to information you have sent us, please contact:
